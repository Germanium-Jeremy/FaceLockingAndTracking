1. INTEGRATED SITUATION
1. Project Description

BENAX Technologies Ltd is developing an AI-powered camera control system for live presentations, lectures, performances, and video-conferencing environments, where the camera must automatically follow a designated main subject in real time.

Unlike conventional face tracking systems that follow any detected face, this system is required to lock onto one pre-enrolled speaker identity and continue tracking that same individual even when other faces appear in the camera frame (such as audience members, assistants, or co-presenters).

The project is designed as an integrated assessment, bringing together competencies in machine learning, computer vision, networking, embedded systems, motor control, and power electronics.

1.1 System Overview
- A PC runs the AI pipeline, performing camera input acquisition, face detection, single-identity face recognition, face tracking, and generation of motion-control commands.
- MQTT over Wi-Fi is used to continuously publish control commands from the PC to the embedded system.
- An ESP8266 device subscribes to the MQTT commands and generates motor control signals.
- A servo motor rotates the camera horizontally to keep the speaker centered.

1.2 System Features
a) Face Enrollment (Registration)
The system captures 10–30 facial images of the main speaker under slightly different angles and expressions and generates a single stored facial profile (embedding/template) for later recognition.

b) Single-Identity Recognition ("Speaker Lock")
During operation, the system identifies the enrolled speaker and deliberately ignores all other faces detected in the frame.

c) Tracking and Motion Control
The camera remains centered on the recognized speaker by publishing motion statuses (e.g., MOVED_LEFT, MOVED_RIGHT, CENTERED, STOPPED, OUT_OF_FRAME) to the embedded controller.

d) Robustness and Re-acquisition
If the speaker's face is temporarily occluded (partially or fully blocked from view), the system enters a controlled search mode and re-acquires the same enrolled speaker once visibility is restored.

e) Logging and Evidence
The system records recognition results, including speaker identity, confidence score, and motor control commands, to provide verifiable operational evidence.

TASK
Design, implement, integrate, and demonstrate a complete AI-Powered Single-Speaker Face Recognition and Camera Tracking System with MQTT-based embedded motor control, proper power architecture, safety measures, and verifiable operational logging.

The system must:
- Enroll and recognize a single authorized speaker
- Track the recognized speaker in real time
- Convert tracking error into motor control logic
- Publish and subscribe to commands using MQTT
- Drive a servo motor using ESP8266
- Ensure proper wiring and stable motion
- Log speaker identity, confidence, timestamps, and motor commands
- Demonstrate correct behavior under realistic conditions (multiple faces, occlusion, etc.)
